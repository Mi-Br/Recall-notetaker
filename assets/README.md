# Bot camera tile

`bot-avatar.jpg` is what the notetaker bot shows as its camera feed, both before and while
recording (Recall `automatic_video_output`). Override with `BOT_IMAGE=<path to .jpg>` or turn
it off with `BOT_IMAGE=none`.

Spec: 1280 × 720 opaque RGB JPEG, under 500 KB, face and hair inside the central
720 × 720 square (x = 280–1000) so Google Meet's square-ish tiles don't crop it, no text.

Current image: "Angela", an original adult character, generated by Michail with an image
generator from his approved "Moonlit Muse" reference (October 2026).
